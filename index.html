The real problem

You have bought this before. You still cannot say what it bought you.

Almost every new patient calls the office before they book. Most agencies never track that call. So the report shows up full of impressions and keyword counts, the invoice clears, and nobody in the practice can tell you whether one crown came out of it.

It is worse than it sounds. Most contracts are written around deliverables, so the agency gets paid the same whether your schedule fills or sits empty. Four blog posts went up. That was the promise. Nobody promised patients.

That is a counting problem before it is a marketing problem. Fix the counting and the argument ends.
